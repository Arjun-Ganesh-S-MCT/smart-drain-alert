FlowBeacon Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

FlowBeacon is a smart drainage monitoring desktop application designed to help authorized personnel monitor drainage conditions and identify potential blockages.

2. Information We Collect

FlowBeacon may process sensor readings, drainage monitoring information, device connection information and drainage location information required for its functionality.

The application does not intentionally collect personal information unless such information is provided through a feature that requires it.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by FlowBeacon may be used to:

  • Display drainage conditions.
  • Monitor water flow and sensor readings.
  • Identify potential drainage blockages.
  • Display reported drainage locations.
  • Generate monitoring alerts and reports.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Data storage and transmission depend on the application's configuration and connected monitoring infrastructure. Information is not intentionally sold to third parties.

Any third-party services used by the application may process information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through FlowBeacon. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

If FlowBeacon integrates third-party services, such as map providers or cloud infrastructure, those services may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

7. User Rights and Data Control

Users may contact the FlowBeacon development team to ask about information associated with their use of the application or request assistance with applicable data rights.

8. Children's Privacy

FlowBeacon is designed as a drainage monitoring tool for authorized operational users and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
